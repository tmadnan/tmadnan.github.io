We present a large-scale, systematic benchmark of video foundation models (VFMs) for remote PD screening, built on a novel dataset of $\mathbf{1,888}$ participants ($727$ with PD) comprising $\mathbf{32,847}$ videos across $16$ standardized clinical tasks. Evaluating seven state-of-the-art VFMs — including VideoPrism, V-JEPA, ViViT, and VideoMAE — we find VideoPrism excels at visual speech kinematics and facial expressivity, V-JEPA is superior for upper-limb motor tasks, and TimeSformer remains highly competitive for rhythmic tasks like finger tapping, with AUCs of $\mathbf{76.4\text{–}85.3\%}$ across tasks.
