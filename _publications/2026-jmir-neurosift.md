We introduce **NeuroSift**, a task-aware, interpretable machine learning framework for assessing recording quality and task compliance in home-recorded multimedia data collected for remote Parkinson's disease assessment. Covering three remotely collected tasks — finger-tapping, facial expression (smile), and speech (pangram utterance) — NeuroSift's quality classification models achieved $\mathbf{77\text{–}90\%}$ ordinal accuracy against expert consensus, supporting more reliable downstream screening in unsupervised, at-home settings.
